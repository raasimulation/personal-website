Chameleon
Publication, Print, Poster2025
Chameleon is a publication produced by the VCU Graphic Design MFA students in their second year. Each year a color is chosen and each student writes using that color as a guide. In 2025, the color was Orange 021U. This year's publication was printed on newsprint, 18 x 24 inches.
